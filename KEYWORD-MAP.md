## Ownership rules

- The homepage owns the branded Firecrawl tools hub intent; `/firecrawl` owns the explanatory product overview intent. Both pages use `firecrawl` as the entity term, while their H1s, introductions, and next steps remain distinct.
- `firecrawl pricing`, `firecrawl api`, `firecrawl mcp`, and `firecrawl api key` each have one dedicated route. Related variants stay secondary on that route.
- `url extractor`, `llms.txt generator`, and the three Markdown queries belong to working tool pages, not generic guide pages.
- `firecrawl self-hosted` is the readable page target; the supplied `firecrawl self host` wording remains a supporting variant.
- `firecrawl login`, misspellings such as `fireclaw` or `firecrwal`, and official blog topics such as AnyDoc, Codex, Kimi, and Hermes are not targets for this independent site.
